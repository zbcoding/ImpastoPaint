The psd-*.psd and psd-*.psb files in this folder come from the psd-tools test corpus
(https://github.com/psd-tools/psd-tools/tree/main/tests/psd_files). Each psd-*.expected.png is
the merged composite Photoshop saved inside the matching file. Most keep their corpus name after
the psd- prefix; the refused/converted fixtures were renamed for what they test:

  psd-refused-16bit.psd                  16bit5x5.psd
  psd-refused-32bit.psd                  32bit5x5.psd
  psd-refused-cmyk.psd                   cmyk-gray-ramp.psd (image resources removed)
  psd-refused-large-document.psb         1layer.psb
  psd-converted-clipping-blend-modes.psd clipping-mask4.psd
  psd-converted-adjustment-text.psd      clip-adjustment.psd
  psd-converted-layer-effects.psd        effect-stroke-gradient.psd (image resources removed)
  psd-converted-grayscale.psd            gray0.psd (image resources removed)

"Image resources removed" means the file's image resources section (ICC profile, thumbnail,
XMP metadata) was emptied to keep the fixture small; the layer and pixel data are Photoshop's.
